Layoutsmart

Outsmart your desktop.

Layoutsmart remembers where your windows are and puts them back with one click — for example after you plug in or unplug a display.

This installer:

  • puts Layoutsmart in your Applications folder and starts it (look for its icon in the menu bar);
  • adds the layoutsmart command for use in Terminal.

Nothing else is installed, and Layoutsmart never connects to the internet.
