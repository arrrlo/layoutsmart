One more step

Layoutsmart is now running — its icon is in the menu bar.

To see and move other apps' windows, macOS needs your permission:

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  2. Switch on Layoutsmart.

Then arrange your windows, click the menu bar icon and choose Save Window Positions….

Layoutsmart starts automatically when you log in. You can change that in System Settings → General → Login Items.
